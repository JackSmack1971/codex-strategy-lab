# Alpha Discovery

Define mechanism, participants, horizon and why the effect might persist after costs. Compare competing explanations and prior empirical evidence. Identify observable falsifiers and necessary data; do not treat plausible mechanisms as measured returns. Hand off to hypothesis-engineering.

Return evidence, limitations, and next safe action. Follow AGENTS.md; this skill grants no authority.
